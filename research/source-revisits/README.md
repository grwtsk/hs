# Source revisits: a changed source is a new reading obligation

This is the bounded resumption practice requested in
[Hs #75](https://github.com/grwtsk/hs/issues/75). It applies to the actual Huey
manuscript and to every source used by Hs. It is not an unattended watcher.

The instruction was: “agree. Mark an issue to revisit the Huey manuscript (and
any source) as they evolve. Merge. advance.” Agreement is recorded in the context
of the preceding RG-01-R02 work and PR #73. It does not silently accept the whole
book, every proposed scholarly interpretation, a standing merge delegation, or
G1, G2 or G3. The instruction to revisit sources is already given; do not ask the
author to authorize that same preparation again.

## When to revisit

During an authorized task, revisit the sources on which its material claims rely.
Check for a newly supplied version, an upstream correction, a changed permission
or a difference discovered while reading. Recheck relevant sources before a
dependent passage, contract or edition is accepted. Do not make all sources in
all repositories settle before an unrelated writing task can proceed.

No recurring schedule, webhook, polling process, paid service, unsolicited
reminder or external contact is installed by these records. Source reading and
source disclosure remain different permissions.

## What to read and record

Identify the prior inspected version and its exact read scope. Establish the
currently available version and inspect the changed passages with sufficient
context. For a Git file, retain the repository, commit, path and blob identity.
For an issue, retain its URL, relevant comment IDs and the actual inspected
revision metadata. For a publication, retain edition, section/page and corrections.
A moving link, a catalog entry or an updated timestamp is not an inspected text.

Classify the delta as unchanged, newly relevant, corrected, superseded for a stated
purpose, contested, unavailable/withheld, or not yet re-examined. Keep the source's
claim separate from the reviewer's inference. Newness does not itself prove truth
or refute an earlier source. Record creation, event, inclusion in an issued record,
receipt, discovery and review times separately where they matter; do not make a
later remedy erase the history it remedies.

Trace the delta to the affected Hs passages, claims, definitions, RG/DG cases,
contracts, citations and tests. A stable passage name is not an exact proposition
revision. Preserve prior readings and corrections; amend the derived account,
not the original source. Reassess only the affected approvals and scope.

## The manuscript is not its progress report

Revisit the Huey manuscript itself through an authorized source channel when the
relevant text is available. Reading an issue about a corrected chapter is not
reading that chapter. A report of successful tests is not a replay. A merged
instruction can be available even while a private manuscript is not inspected.
Record these statuses independently; do not collapse them into one fresh badge.

The first record here re-examines the public RF-01 contract only. It does not
certify the current private manuscript, all of Huey, or the continuing adequacy of
every R02 source. The outstanding manuscript reading is explicit rather than
being reconstructed from a summary or treated as nonexistent.

## Rigor during a revisit

Retain the current Huey relational purpose: people and supplied identities,
observations, available accounts, selection, omission, transmission and authority.
Do not substitute an accusation of racism, exoneration, a motive prerequisite,
identity erasure or a role-based credibility ranking. An original source may use
or contest a classification; preserve its exact proposition and grounds. A
supported relation remains describable while another link is unknown.

Apply the same scrutiny to Hs's favored reading and to contrary evidence. A
correction may change a particular relation without nullifying every account or
establishing a universal verdict. Preserve material disagreement and its actual
human owner. No source hash, human approval or editorial preference supplies a
missing proof, historical fact, third-party assent or test result.

## Records and handoff

[index.json](index.json) lists actual records and their dependent work.
[huey-rf01-001.json](huey-rf01-001.json) is the first bounded review. Later reviews
receive new IDs and reference predecessors; do not overwrite this record to make
an earlier inspection seem more extensive. Record further actual manuscript and
primary-source readings as they occur, without retaining restricted particulars
in public metadata. Research access does not authorize corpus copying or training.

Integrate these records with the source/claim ledger in #14, source register #64,
and resumption process #5/#63 when those deliverables are implemented. This change
does not claim to implement their general validators or the interface gates.

An individual record can finish with a precise unchanged finding or a scoped
remaining obligation. The continuing parent #75 stays open until the author
retires or replaces it. Research and provisional prose can advance; consequential
new disclosure, rights, retention, outside contact, spending and publication keep
their existing human boundaries. One bounded work PR at a time remains the rule.
