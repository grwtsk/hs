# NAV-01: the page is the input

Status: source-grounded design revision and agent explanation, not an implemented
interface or accepted book edition. Source: [HS-NAV-01](../../sources/conversation/03-edit-navigation.md),
with exact passage ranges in its manifest. Tracking: Hs issues #79 and #80.

## What changes

The person has already expressed something by changing the page. Requiring the
same intention again in an intensity setting would make the interface's own
organization an additional task. The edit should carry both the expression and
its authorized route. Intensity describes the change; it is not a second object
that the person must configure.

This replaces the earlier mandatory separate composer. It does not reinterpret
that earlier source as if it had always meant the later design. The original
captures stay unchanged. A click on the sphere now requests discovery rather
than composition. Its hold/move/release meanings remain stable, including the
independence of volume, density, typography and color.

The correction is already an instruction, not an unresolved poll. The exact
implementation, genuinely open consent questions, and the physical connection
still have their respective research and human-review obligations. See #8,
#9, #10, #65 and #66; G1 #36 and G2 #38 remain prerequisites to implementation.

## From a word to a place

The author's `/apple` example begins with the familiar fruit interpretation in
the absence of a contrary user instruction. Other senses and their historical
or cultural settings remain available through links. `/apple/tree` refines the
inquiry without requiring a second input surface.

An apple tree identifies a kind of thing in the inquiry. That apple tree points
toward a situated candidate. The apple tree can become a shared reference when
people have enough common context to identify it. These descriptions explain a
possible progression, not a universal linguistic rule or evidence that a concrete
tree has already been identified.

The cache can carry the work making that specificity possible: observations,
identifiers, place and time where permitted, who supplied the relation, and
competing identifications. A definite article cannot do all that work alone.
Nor does a shared referent imply ownership of the tree or permission to disclose
its precise location. Familiarity is a relation to a context, not a global vote
on what a word must mean.

Geographic availability and culture expressed in use can contribute within the
person's actual user-agent permissions. The cache must not infer a demographic
identity from location or use accumulated interactions for an ungranted purpose.
Withdrawing context permission should leave general navigation available.

## Discovery without a compulsory question box

A sphere click can begin with `/hello`. The person can author a Hola projection
at `/hola`; translation is a versioned relation, not a silent replacement of the
original or automatic public contribution. Subsequent deliberate clicks can ask
natural questions and move through available grwtsk content. The following
illustrative sequence is agent-proposed, not an author-selected script:

- What is here?
- What does this refer to?
- What supports that account?

Each step needs actual material, an intelligible relation to the previous step,
and a way to stop or return. The interface must not invent availability to keep
the sequence moving. Discovery follows the click; the cache does not activate it
merely because there is more to show. Releasing a manipulation must not also
count as a discovery click.

## Editing and following are both real capabilities

Links are text. Their local rendering can be edited while their destination can
be followed. Indicate editable-only, navigable-only and combined capabilities in
visible, non-color-only form and programmatic semantics. Do not turn the same
undifferentiated action into both caret placement and navigation. Exact controls
remain an implementation question, not a reason to restore the intensity box.

The local draft, generated address, committed navigation and publication remain
different effects. They need not become four screens. An authorized semantic
edit/submit action can carry the route, while incomplete typing, IME composition,
dictation correction or autosave does not unexpectedly take the person away.
A permitted local preview can refine the view without discarding the active edit.

W3C's guidance supplies constraints for evaluating this behavior: predictable
context changes, identifiable link purposes, and programmatically determinable
component names, roles and values. It does not select the complete interaction
pattern for Hs or establish that the proposed interface already conforms.

## Resistance has a reason and an end

The model may expose an uncertain referent, a competing sense, an unsupported
inference or a disclosure consequence. Its advice should preserve the edit and
make a useful alternative available. The human can decline that advice and
retain a permitted local rendering or under-refined route. Repeated pressure to
accept the model's preferred reading would not be yielding agency.

Yielding does not make a changed claim proved, overwrite another author's source,
or bypass a disclosure boundary. The system must carry the expression and the
record of its grounds separately. The rigorous account under #74 is not weakened
by a gentler rendering, and a stronger rendering acquires no extra evidence.

All voices can contribute. Wider transmission is separately accountable to the
human's authorization and the actual grounds of the contribution. Copies retain
common lineage rather than becoming independent corroboration. The proposed
force/mass/gap relationship is examined in the companion mathematical note, not
used as a covert influence score or participation barrier.
