# Disagreements remain part of the writing

These are sourced tensions and agent-formulated alternatives, not claims that
named humans have quarreled. No position is adopted here. A later disposition applies only to its recorded
scope; other affected work is not silently settled. Research and provisional
writing may proceed; affected promised implementation requires scoped disposition.
The records are in [research/disagreements.yaml](../../research/disagreements.yaml).
